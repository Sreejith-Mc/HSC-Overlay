What the engine works out for you

  • K / D / A, first bloods and alive counts — from kills you log.
  • Credits — kill rewards, win bonus, loss streak bonus, plant bonus, 9 000 cap.
  • Loadout value — weapons + shields + ability charges, per player and per team.
  • Clutches — the system flags a 1vX the moment it happens and awards it if that player's team wins.
  • Thrifties — the winning team was out-bought at round start (ratio or flat gap, both configurable).
  • Flawless, aces, pistol rounds, plants and defuses.
  • Buy phase auto-rolls into live when the clock runs out. Side swap fires at the half and each OT pair.
